# 07 Growth, Listing and Launch: HireKit

## Listing copy (draft; adapt to the Listing Fields docs)
Name: HireKit
Tagline: Code review for your job application.
Short description: Paste a job and your CV. HireKit tests every requirement against your real experience, shows a coverage score, rewrites your CV as reviewable diffs, and writes a cover letter and interview prep using only what you can prove. Track every application in one place.
Long description points: requirements as tests with Pass, Partial or Fail; evidence IDs on every line; resume diff view; keyword and ATS lint; claim risk and the questions you should expect; gap plan with honest phrasing; tracker; your data stays in your Anna account and can be deleted anytime.
Category/tags: Productivity, Developer Tools, Career, AI Agents.
Screenshots: Report (dark), Resume diff (dark), Needs your input, Tracker (light), Cover letter.

## Positioning
"Other tools write what sounds good. HireKit shows what you can prove." For developers: "Test your CV against the job before a recruiter does."

## Audience channels (real users, genuine use)
Developer and tech communities: CS and engineering departments, bootcamp cohorts, Google Developer Group and similar chapters, hackathon Discords (including the DoraHacks and Anna communities), tech Twitter/X and LinkedIn, dev.to and Hashnode posts, Reddit career and developer communities where self-promotion rules allow, GitHub README of the project, short demo videos (screen recording a real job post becoming a coverage report in 45 seconds).
Timing: graduate and internship application windows; layoff and hiring news cycles; post when programmes open or close.

## Rolling plan (starts the day of approval; prepare during review)
- Pre-launch (11-10 to approval): 10 to 15 real developers and CS students do a private beta on their own accounts with a job they actually care about; collect testimonials and bugs; prepare demo video, 3 post templates, one-pager.
- Launch day: post in the owner's communities, ask each person to try it on one real job post, share the link. Answer questions personally.
- Days 2 to 7: daily post (before/after example, "5 gaps recruiters notice", walkthrough). Ask satisfied users to share with one friend.
- Days 8 to 15: partner with 3 to 5 campus groups or creators for a short "tailor your application" session using the app live.
- Final 5 days: gap check against target, extra sessions.
Target pace: 14+ new qualified users a day after approval; aim for 300 total for buffer.

## Fair-play guardrails (read before recruiting helpers)
Helpers must use their own account and a genuine job post. Do not pay, tip, or give prizes for runs. Do not ask people to generate throwaway runs. Do not run it yourself repeatedly or on test accounts. Encourage real feedback instead of "please click".

## Tracking
Maintain `growth-log.csv` (date, channel, action, estimated reach, signups reported). Anna's server logs are the truth; check Developer Console analytics if V10 confirms.
